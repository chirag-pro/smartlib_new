About SmartLib

A fully functional Library Management System built with Flask.

Our Mission

Empowering Students Through Knowledge

SmartLib is a comprehensive Library Management System designed to make library operations efficient, transparent, and accessible for students and faculty alike.

It demonstrates core concepts of full-stack web development including user authentication, database design, CRUD operations, and role-based access control.
